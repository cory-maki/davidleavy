David C. Leavy and the Operational Architecture of CNN Worldwide

📅 September 14, 2026
✎ David Leavy
⌚ 2 min read

When CNN Worldwide created the position of chief operating officer in June 2023, it chose David C. Leavy, a Washington, D.C.-based executive with more than two decades inside Discovery and Warner Bros. Discovery, to fill it.

The appointment placed commercial, revenue, operational, technology and promotional functions under one leader. For a news organization whose brand rests on its journalism, the role was designed around everything that keeps that journalism funded, distributed and visible. David Leavy's mandate also kept a line back to the parent company: he continued to oversee public policy and social responsibility for Warner Bros. Discovery.

A portfolio built for breadth

The notable feature of the CNN remit was its width. It combined the revenue side of the business with the technology that delivers the product and the promotional work that brings audiences to it. Each of those functions answers to different measures of success, runs on different timelines and speaks a different internal language.

Holding them together depends less on any single decision than on structure: who reports to whom, which priorities take precedence when budgets and timelines collide, and how a decision made in one group is understood in another. That kind of coordination also defined his record of Discovery milestones, from a capital-markets listing to a streaming launch.

The role was built around everything that keeps journalism funded, distributed and visible.

Institutional knowledge as an operating asset

Leavy did not arrive at CNN as an outsider. As Chief Corporate Affairs Officer of Warner Bros. Discovery, he had already worked across the combined company's government relations, public policy, social responsibility and corporate marketing. Before that, as Chief Corporate Operations Officer at Discovery Inc., he had been involved in the company's most significant strategic initiatives.

That history matters in an operating role. Running cross-functional teams inside a large media group depends on relationships and on an understanding of how the parent company makes decisions. An executive who already knows where those decisions are made can move a shared priority through an organization more directly than one learning its structure for the first time.

Communications discipline in an operating role

The approach also reflects where his career began. Before Discovery, Leavy served as chief spokesman for the National Security Council in the Clinton White House, an environment in which communications carried consequences well beyond the commercial. His early years in public service established a habit of precision that carries into operational work, where how a decision is explained can matter as much as the decision itself.

The same discipline applies to promotion and brand work at a global news network, where messaging has to hold together across platforms, markets and audiences. In Leavy's remit, the promotional function sat alongside the commercial and technology portfolios rather than apart from them, so that what CNN said about itself and how it ran its business were managed from the same seat.

Conclusion

The CNN Worldwide COO role was a test of operational breadth: commercial, revenue, technology and promotional work held under one executive. It also drew on the full arc of Leavy's career, from government communications to corporate affairs to company-wide operations, which is set out in David Leavy’s professional biography.

David Leavy

Senior Media Executive

David Leavy is a senior media executive whose career runs from the National Security Council press office in the Clinton White House, through more than two decades at Discovery and Warner Bros. Discovery, to the chief operating officer role at CNN Worldwide.

Washington, D.C.

Profile

An executive built across disciplines

David C. Leavy has spent his career where communications, policy and operations meet. Based in Washington, D.C., he began in government as chief spokesman for the National Security Council, then spent more than twenty years at Discovery Inc. and Warner Bros. Discovery, where his portfolio widened to span corporate affairs, government relations, marketing and operations.

At Discovery he worked on a sequence of company-defining moves: the 2008 NASDAQ listing, the acquisition of Scripps Networks Interactive, the European Olympic rights agreement with Eurosport and the 2021 launch of discovery+. In June 2023 he was appointed chief operating officer of CNN Worldwide, a remit set out in his operating role at CNN Worldwide.

Outside the corporate sphere, Leavy serves on the Board of Trustees at Colby College and co-chairs the Board of Trustees at the Salisbury School. His career in media and public service reads as a single arc, with each stage building on the one before it.

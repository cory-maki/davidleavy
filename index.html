David C. Leavy

Operational Strategist & Media Executive

David C. Leavy is Chief Operating Officer of CNN Worldwide, where he oversees commercial, revenue, operational, technology, and promotional operations across the network's global footprint. A 25-year veteran of Discovery Inc. and Warner Bros. Discovery, David Leavy has spent his career at the operational center of consequential media transactions and platform launches.

New York, NY · United States

Profile

A career built on operational execution.

Across two and a half decades in global media, David C. Leavy has been the executive who translates strategy into the machinery that runs underneath it — the deal sequencing, the cross-functional coordination, the operational discipline that determines whether a transaction or a platform launch actually lands.

At Warner Bros. Discovery, where David Leavy served as Chief Corporate Affairs Officer before moving into the COO seat at CNN Worldwide, he managed an unusually broad portfolio: corporate relations, global government relations and public policy, communications, marketing, research, and social responsibility. Earlier, as Chief Corporate Operating Officer of Discovery, he was central to the company's 2021 launch of discovery+, its 2008 NASDAQ listing, and its 2018 acquisition of Scripps Networks Interactive.

His foundation was built outside the media industry entirely. Before joining Discovery, Leavy served as Chief Spokesman and Senior Director of Public Affairs for the National Security Council in the Clinton White House — an institutional credential that has shaped how he operates inside large organizations to this day. More detail on the full career arc is available on the about page, including his executive profile on F6S.
